About Me

I am Aaron J Evan 'Jet' Tolentino. I am a professional musician of twenty years, specializing in piano and sacred music. Currently, I serve as a Transactions Specialist within the Auto Finance division at JPMorgan Chase in Manila, managing high-value financial operations. Outside of my professional commitments, I am an avid volleyball player and a supporter of both local and international live performances. I look forward to our collaboration and am committed to our collective growth and success. I truly believe that with dedication and faith, we can overcome any challenge together.
